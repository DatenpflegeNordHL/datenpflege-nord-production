## Rejected / boundary cases

- Managed IT, IT-Support, PC repair, network administration and Microsoft-365 support: **REJECT** as standalone target categories without new business proof.
- SEO/GEO agency, performance marketing, social media and generic advertising agency: **REJECT** as standalone product categories under current business truth.
- Generic “KI Beratung” can remain a keyword candidate, but should not outrank implementation-specific positioning unless service proof expands.
- Pure “Softwareentwickler + Ort” should be treated carefully because SERPs can be job-dominant.
