## Deterministic routing of the complete raw cluster set

The 56 raw cluster headings are research groupings, not 57 page proposals. Route SOFTWARE_LUEBECK and INTEGRATION_LUEBECK to S; WEB_LUEBECK to W except business-logic Webanwendungen to S; AI_LUEBECK, AUTOMATION_LUEBECK and N8N_LUEBECK to A. BRAND routes to `/`. BUSINESS_FIT_UNSICHER is BLOCK.

For every family: `_COMMERCIAL` terms support the existing family owner without implying national reach; `_COMPARISON`, `_COST`, `_DECISION`, `_PROBLEM_SOLUTION`, `_USE_CASES` are supporting sections or HOLD assets as detailed in GOLDEN-AUTHORITY-CONTENT-MAP. `_HAMBURG` is HOLD on service-area truth; `_KIEL` is HOLD on distinct local value; `_SH` is service-area support with standalone pages HOLD. All N8N city/state page variants are BLOCK. No orphan raw cluster grants a BUILD permission.

## Unique value and cannibalization acceptance for this expansion

Software adds a standard/extension/custom decision table, project input checklist and integration failure questions. Web adds build/modernize/landingpage choices and customer preparation. AI adds deterministic vs LLM vs approval boundaries. They solve different buyer decisions and cross-link instead of duplicating API tutorials on all pages. No keywords are quotas; broad head terms do not become page titles solely for volume. Future split decisions must update this map and GSC ownership evidence first.
