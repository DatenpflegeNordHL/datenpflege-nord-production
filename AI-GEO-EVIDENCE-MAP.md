## Technical and entity prerequisites

Google states that its AI search features use normal SEO foundations: indexed/snippet-eligible pages, accessible text, useful links, accurate structured data and consistent business details; no special AI files/schema are needed. Traffic from those features is included in GSC Web reports. See [Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

Reject city/keyword swaps and scaled filler; Google's [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) describe doorway/scaled-content and link-spam concerns. These safeguards are user-value requirements, not a keyword density formula.

Live baseline robots includes Cloudflare-managed content signals and named crawler blocks beyond repository robots. Record and review these under the deployment contract; do not infer that all AI search is blocked or silently relax training preferences. HTTP, canonical and crawler-specific access must be checked separately. No ai.txt, new AI schema, artificial FAQ markup or sameAs expansion is implemented.
