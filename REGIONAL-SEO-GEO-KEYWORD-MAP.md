## 2. Non-negotiable regional SEO rules

1. No city/region page is created merely because a keyword has volume.
2. No fake office/address/local schema is allowed for Kiel, Hamburg or any other city.
3. Near-identical city pages are prohibited. Google explicitly identifies substantially similar city/region pages that funnel users to the same destination as doorway abuse.
4. Do not stuff pages with city lists. Geography must be useful to the reader and consistent with actual service delivery.
5. A new location URL requires: real service-area truth, distinct SERP intent/opportunity, unique local value, useful local proof/context, conversion fit and non-cannibalization.
6. `LocalBusiness` addresses represent real locations, not service areas. Remote/on-site service coverage belongs in truthful visible content and appropriate schema/entity fields, not invented addresses.
7. Regional service pages and national knowledge content have different jobs: local pages generate qualified leads; supporting content builds topical authority, links and AI-citation potential.

## 4. Regional competitor / SERP observations

### Lübeck

Software SERP is mixed with jobs and education. Genuine commercial competitors include RXM, EXORD and Software-and-Testing. Webdesign SERPs contain strongly location-focused providers and local directories. Current examples include Webdesign Lübeck / WEDEON, Northbay, Jamp and multiple directory surfaces.

Implication: service copy alone is insufficient. Local proof, reviews/entity signals, clear service scope and external citations are material competitive factors.

### Kiel

Current web/software market contains strong local specialists such as vektorrausch, Webagentur Kiel and Naymspace. Current KI SERPs also contain dedicated Kiel pages from national/specialist providers plus local AI/digital competitors.

Implication: Kiel has enough demand and competitive structure to deserve its own acquisition strategy, but a future Kiel page must contain unique service value and truthful regional proof rather than a Lübeck page with the city name swapped.

### Hamburg

Hamburg has much larger demand but materially heavier competition across software, web and AI. Current examples include CQUELLE for individual software and multiple dedicated AI/web agencies. Directory density is also much higher.

Implication: Hamburg should be treated as a separate expansion market. A Hamburg landing page is not approved until service-area/business truth is explicitly aligned and the page can carry distinct Hamburg-facing proof/value.

### Schleswig-Holstein

The strongest directly measured state-modified opportunity in this pass is `webdesign schleswig-holstein` (~110, KD 28). Regional SERPs include statewide agency pages and marketplaces.

Implication: Schleswig-Holstein is strategically important as the service-area/entity layer. A dedicated SH page remains HOLD pending SERP-overlap and uniqueness review against Lübeck/Kiel content.
