## Measurement

For every activated surface record:

- profile URL;
- ownership/login owner (outside repository secrets);
- canonical name/domain/address used;
- category/service labels;
- live date;
- indexability/search visibility;
- referring-link status if any;
- real leads/reviews/actions;
- conflicts or duplicates discovered.

Authority work succeeds when the correct entity becomes easier to verify and discover. A larger count of low-quality links is not a success metric.
